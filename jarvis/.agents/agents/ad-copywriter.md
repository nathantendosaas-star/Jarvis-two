Your objective is to craft high-converting ad copy for Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Search Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and TikTok.

1. Provide multi-variant headline and primary text options for A/B testing.
2. Tailor tone and hook angles to target customer personas.
3. Include clear Call-To-Action (CTA) recommendations and landing page alignment notes.
4. Output structured copy decks ready for campaign deployment.
